Copyright

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Ownership

The Lets Connect name, logo, design, text and software are owned by the Lets Connect team. All rights reserved. You may view the platform and share links to it, but you may not copy its design or code without permission.

Content added by organisers

  • Organisers keep ownership of the posters, images and descriptions they upload.
  • By uploading content, an organiser allows Lets Connect to display it on the platform and in links shared from it.
  • Organisers must only upload content they own or have permission to use. Do not upload other people's photos, posters, music or artwork without permission.

Reporting infringement

If you believe something on Lets Connect copies your work without permission, email support@letsconnect.example (replace with your real address) with:

  • a description of your work and where it appears on Lets Connect (the page address),
  • proof that you own it or act for the owner,
  • your name and contact details.

We will review the report and may remove the content while we do. This process follows the principles of the Copyright Act, 2001 (Kenya).
