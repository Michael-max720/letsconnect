Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Who we are

Lets Connect is the data controller for the personal information described here. You can contact us at support@letsconnect.example (replace with your real address).

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, optional username, account type and a password (stored only as a one-way hash, never as plain text).
  • Tickets and payments: the tickets you buy, amounts paid, the M-Pesa phone number used and the payment reference. We never see your M-Pesa PIN. You enter it on your phone.
  • Activity: the organisers you follow and the updates they send.
  • On your device: a session cookie that keeps you logged in, and your display choices (theme, text size, language, region).

Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and verify your email.
  • To issue, check and honour tickets, and to process payments.
  • To show you updates from organisers you follow.
  • To keep the platform safe, fix problems and improve it.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

Who can see it

  • Organisers can see ticket sales for their own events and how many followers they have. They cannot see who their followers are.
  • Gate agents check tickets at the entrance.
  • Administrators review organiser accounts and keep the platform running.
  • Payment provider: M-Pesa (Safaricom) processes payments.

We do not sell your personal information.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 (Kenya) you have the right to be told how your data is used, to see the data we hold about you, to object to its use, and to ask us to correct or delete it. To use these rights, email us at the address above. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

Children

Accounts are for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Some events are family friendly, and adults may buy tickets for children to attend with them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For more detail on what we store and for how long, see the Data Handling page.
