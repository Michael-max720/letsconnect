Data Handling

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This page explains, in more detail than the Privacy Policy, what data the platform stores, who can see it and how long it is kept.

What stays on your device

  • A session cookie that keeps you logged in (it expires after 8 hours).
  • Your display choices: theme, brightness, text size, layout, language and region.
  • The time you last opened your updates, used to show the unread count.

You can clear these at any time in your browser settings, or with "Reset to defaults" in Settings.

How we protect it

  • Passwords are hashed, so even we cannot read them.
  • What you can see and do depends on your account type. Attendees, organisers, gate agents and administrators each have separate permissions, checked on the server.
  • The platform is designed to be served over HTTPS when it goes live.

Sharing

We share data only where needed to run the service: with M-Pesa (Safaricom) to take payment, with the organiser of an event to run that event, and with our hosting provider once the platform is deployed. We do not sell personal data.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data, except records we must keep for accounting or legal reasons.

If something goes wrong

If a data breach affects your personal information, we will tell the people affected and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner as the Data Protection Act, 2019 requires.
